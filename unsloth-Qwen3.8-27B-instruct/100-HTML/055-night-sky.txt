Creative Prompt:
Create a starry night sky with constellation lines. Stars have color temperature variation (blue-white to orange-red), twinkle animation, and glow for bright stars. Constellations are automatically generated by connecting nearby stars with faint lines. Moving the mouse near constellation stars highlights the constellation with brighter lines and shows a label. The background is very dark blue with trailing fade. The overall aesthetic is astronomical, serene, and educational — like a portable planetarium.

Thinking effort: high

Techniques used:
- Canvas 2D star rendering with blackbody color temperature mapping (Kelvin → sRGB)
- Sine-based per-star twinkle with randomized phase/speed/amplitude
- Radial gradient glow for bright stars + diffraction spikes for the brightest
- Nearest-neighbor constellation generation (4–9 stars each, connected edges)
- Mouse proximity detection with smooth highlight interpolation
- CSS-positioned constellation labels with text-shadow glow
- Trailing background fade via semi-transparent fillRect each frame
- Responsive: dynamic star regeneration on resize (debounced), DPR-aware canvas
- Touch support for mobile
