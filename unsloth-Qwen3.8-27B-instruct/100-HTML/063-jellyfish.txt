Creative Prompt: Bioluminescent Jellyfish Visualization
========================================================

Create a bioluminescent jellyfish visualization with translucent bells and flowing tentacles.
Jellyfish drift slowly upward with pulsing bells and undulating tentacles. Each jellyfish has a
blue/cyan hue with radial gradient transparency. The bell has an inner pattern. Tentacles are
drawn as segmented curves with sine-based wave motion. Tiny bubbles float upward. The background
is deep ocean blue with trailing fade. Clicking adds a new jellyfish. The overall aesthetic is
aquatic, ethereal, and luminous — like deep-sea bioluminescent creatures.

Model: Qwen3.8-27B-instruct
Thinking effort: high

Techniques used:
- Canvas 2D rendering with elliptical bell shapes and multi-stop radial gradients for translucency
- Segmented tentacle curves using quadratic Bézier paths with sine-wave animation per segment
- Bell pulse animation via sinusoidal radius modulation
- Upward drift with sinusoidal lateral movement and screen-edge wrapping
- Inner bell pattern: concentric luminous rings, central nucleus glow, radial filaments
- Oral arms (thicker central tentacles) in addition to outer tentacles
- Bioluminescent dot spots along tentacles with independent flicker
- Bubble particle system with wobble and highlight
- Subtle volumetric light rays (caustic effect) from above
- Trailing background fade (semi-transparent overlay each frame)
- Jellyfish pool management (max 12, oldest removed on overflow)
- Click/touch to spawn new jellyfish with bubble burst
- Depth sorting by scale for layered rendering
- DPR-aware canvas scaling for crisp rendering on retina displays
- HSL color space for natural blue/cyan hue variation per jellyfish
