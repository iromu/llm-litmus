Creative Prompt:
Create a fantasy landscape of floating islands suspended in a misty sky. Each island has a grassy top, rocky bottom with hanging roots, trees (trunk + foliage clusters), and optional small water features. Islands float at different heights and drift gently with sinusoidal vertical motion. A fog layer adds atmospheric depth. The color palette is earthy greens, browns, and cool blues. Clicking regenerates the island configuration. The overall aesthetic is dreamy, fantastical, and atmospheric — like a scene from a Studio Ghibli film.

- Sinusoidal floating animation with per-island phase/speed/amplitude for independent bobbing
- Hanging root lines via quadratic bezier curves
- Shadow ellipses below each island for grounding
- Depth sorting by Y position (far-to-near draw order)
- Layered fog bands using radial gradients plus horizontal mist strips
- Drifting procedural clouds (puff-based ellipses) split into far/near layers
- Floating pollen/firefly particles with flicker
- Soft sun glow via large radial gradient
- Click-based full-scene regeneration with seeded RNG (mulberry32) and fade-in transition
- DPR-aware canvas sizing for crisp rendering on retina displays
