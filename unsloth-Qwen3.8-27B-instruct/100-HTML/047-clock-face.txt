Create a minimalist analog clock face with glowing blue elements on a dark background. The clock features hour markers (with longer marks at 12, 3, 6, 9), minute dots, hour numbers at cardinal positions, hour/minute/second hands, a glowing second hand with trail, and a center dot. A digital time display sits below the clock face. The clock tells real time. The overall aesthetic is clean, luminous, and precise — like a premium smartwatch face.

- Hands rendered with varying widths/opacities and canvas shadowBlur glow; red-accented second hand with a segmented fading arc trail and counterweight dot
- Static face cached to an offscreen canvas (redrawn only on resize) so the per-frame cost is just the hands
- Digital HH:MM:SS + day/date readout below the face, updated once per second, tabular numerals with text-shadow glow
