Folding Fan — Creative Prompt Artifact
=======================================

Creative prompt:
Create animated folding fans that open and close gently. Each fan has multiple
ribs (spokes) with fabric panels between them, rendered in warm hues. The fan
opens and closes with a sine-wave animation. Ribs are drawn as lines with
varying width and color, and fabric panels are semi-transparent filled
triangles. A pivot dot and handle complete the design. Clicking regenerates
the fans. The background is dark with trailing fade. The overall aesthetic is
elegant, traditional, and kinetic — like hand-painted Japanese fans.

Model: Qwen3.8-27B-instruct
Thinking effort: high

Techniques used:
- Canvas 2D fan rendering with trigonometric rib placement (cos/sin around a
  pivot), rib count randomized 7–12 per fan.
- Radial fan fabric panels as semi-transparent filled triangles between
  adjacent ribs, shaded with radial gradients (darker toward the pivot) and
  subtle per-panel alpha shimmer.
- Rib lines with per-rib linear gradients and varying width (thicker mid-ribs,
  thinner edge ribs) plus small bead caps at the tips.
- Sine-wave open/close animation: each fan's angular span oscillates between a
  near-closed angle and a fully-open span via a cosine-driven 0..1 oscillator
  with per-fan phase and speed.
- Pivot dot rendered as a brass-like radial-gradient sphere with specular
  highlight; handle drawn as a tapered stem with a cap, oriented opposite the
  fan opening.
- Decorative painted border arcs near the rim for a hand-painted feel.
- Click (pointerdown) regeneration with new hues, positions, sizes, and
  orientations; fan count adapts to viewport width.
- Trailing background fade via a translucent dark wash each frame instead of
  a full clear, combined with 'lighter' composite mode for luminous warmth.
- Warm traditional palette: vermilion, persimmon, amber/gold, and rosewood
  hue bands.
- Responsive: devicePixelRatio-aware canvas sizing, fan count scales with
  screen size, hint text uses clamp().
