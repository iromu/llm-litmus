Crystal Growth — Generative Crystallography
=============================================

Creative Prompt:
Create a generative crystal growth visualization where faceted crystal structures
grow organically from nucleation points. Each crystal has multiple facets radiating
from a center, rendered as quadrilateral shapes with varying hues (blue-purple spectrum),
alpha levels, and edge strokes. Crystal tips have a glow effect. Crystals slowly grow
additional facets over time. Clicking spawns new crystals at the cursor. Moving the mouse
also generates new crystals in the vicinity. The background fades with a trailing effect.
The overall aesthetic is frozen, geometric, and luminescent — like observing crystal
formation under a microscope.

Model: Qwen3.8-27B-instruct
Thinking effort: high

Techniques used:
- Canvas quadrilateral rendering for crystal facets (4-point polygon per facet)
- Organic growth algorithm: each crystal spawns child facets adjacent to existing ones,
  with a generation limit of 28 facets per crystal
- HSL color cycling per facet within a blue-purple hue range (220–290°)
- Tip glow via radial gradients at each facet's outer midpoint
- Mouse-following crystal generation (spawn every N frames at cursor ± jitter)
- Trailing background via semi-transparent fillRect (rgba fade, alpha 0.045)
- Click spawning: cluster of 5 crystals at cursor position
- Population control: max 120 crystals; oldest removed when cap reached
- Subtle shimmer animation (per-facet sine pulse on alpha)
- Vignette overlay for depth
- Touch support for mobile
- HUD showing live facet/crystal counts
