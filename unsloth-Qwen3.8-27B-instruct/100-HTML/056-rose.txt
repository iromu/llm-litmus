--- Creative prompt ---
Create a blooming rose with layered petals that open and close gently. The rose has a stem with leaves, and multiple layers of petals in deep red/crimson hues with radial gradient shading. Petals are rendered as ellipses with layer-based depth sorting, creating a 3D flower effect. The bloom animates with a breathing motion. Occasional falling petals drift down. Clicking regenerates the rose. The background is very dark red with trailing fade. The overall aesthetic is romantic, organic, and delicate — like a living rose in the dark.

--- Techniques used ---
- Canvas layered petal rendering with elliptical shapes (ctx.ellipse)
- Depth-sorted rendering: petal rings drawn outer → inner so inner layers overlap correctly
- Radial-gradient petal shading with per-petal hue/saturation/lightness drift around crimson (hsl 348–358°), plus a rim-shadow gradient for depth
- Sine-based breathing bloom: slow global scale oscillation + per-petal wobble phases; ease-out cubic opening animation on spawn
- Stem and leaf drawing with quadratic Bézier curves, thorns, midribs and side veins
- Falling-petal particles: randomized drift, sway, rotation, and alpha decay
- Click-to-regenerate: new random configuration (layer count, petal counts, angles, sizes, hues) with a clean background reset
- Trailing background fade via low-alpha dark-red fill each frame (motion trails)
- Radial glow behind the bloom, tight inner bud core with a slowly rotating spiral hint
- Responsive sizing (min(W,H)-based radius), devicePixelRatio-aware canvas, pointer events
