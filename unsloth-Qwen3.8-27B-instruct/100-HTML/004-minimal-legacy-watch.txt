# 004 — Minimal Legacy Watch

## Creative Prompt
Create a luxury watch product showcase with a vintage editorial aesthetic. A realistic 3D CSS watch face shows an animated working clock with hour, minute, and second hands. The watch has a rose gold body with a cream dial, Roman-style markers (generated by JavaScript), a date window, crown, and leather strap. The layout is a two-column split: the watch on the left with subtle 3D perspective and floating animation, and editorial content on the right including tag, headline, italic description, specification grid, and a call-to-action button. The color palette is warm neutrals, cream, rose gold, and dark brown. Serif typography throughout.

## Model

## Thinking Effort
high

## Techniques Used
- CSS 3D transforms (`perspective`, `preserve-3d`) with a hover-triggered rotation change on the watch body
- Animated floating keyframes plus a breathing floor shadow synced to the float
- Radial/conic gradients for metallic sheen on the rose gold case and brushed ring highlight
- JavaScript-generated watch face: 60 minute ticks and 12 Roman numeral hour markers positioned via rotate/translateY
- Real-time clock driven by `setInterval` (1 s) with millisecond interpolation for smooth second-hand sweep; live date window
- Responsive two-column grid that stacks vertically below 900 px, with reduced watch scale at 480 px
- CTA button hover color inversion (dark brown ↔ cream) with lift and deepened shadow
- Multi-layered box-shadows (inset + drop) for realistic depth on case, dial, crystal, crown, and strap stitching
- `prefers-reduced-motion` support disables the float animation
