Kaleidoscope Flowers — Botanical Psychedelia
=============================================

Creative Prompt:
Create a floral kaleidoscope with blooming flowers that drift and rotate. Each flower has 8 petals drawn with bezier curves, filled with soft radial gradients in shifting hues. Petals bloom open and closed with a sine wave. The center has a glowing core. Clicking adds a new flower at the cursor. Flowers drift slowly with sine-wave motion. The background is dark purple with trailing fade. The overall aesthetic is floral, psychedelic, and delicate — like animated botanical illustrations.

Model: Qwen3.8-27B-instruct
Thinking effort: high

Techniques used:
- Canvas 2D bezier curve petal outlines (two cubic beziers per petal, 8-fold rotational symmetry)
- Multi-petal flowers: outer ring of 8 petals + optional inner counter-rotating ring for depth
- Radial gradient petal fills with progressive hue shift (base → tip), plus subtle vein strokes
- Glowing center core built from concentric circles + pulsing halo (radial gradient)
- Sine-based blooming animation modulating petal length/width over time
- Drift physics: base position + independent sine modulation on X/Y axes at different frequencies
- Slow continuous rotation per flower; per-flower seeded randomness (mulberry32) for variety
- Click/touch to plant a new flower; pool capped at 25 with oldest recycled
- Trailing background fade via low-alpha fill each frame (motion blur trails)
- Subtle vignette overlay for framing; spawn scale-in easing for newly planted flowers
- DPR-aware canvas sizing, pointer events + touch fallback, fully self-contained single file
