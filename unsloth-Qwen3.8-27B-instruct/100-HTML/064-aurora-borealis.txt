Aurora Borealis — Digital Art Gallery Prompt
==============================================

Creative Prompt:
Create an aurora borealis (northern lights) visualization with colorful bands of light rippling across a dark night sky. Multiple aurora bands in green, teal, and purple hues undulate with dual-sine wave motion. Each band has multiple layers with slightly offset positions for depth. Stars twinkle above. A dark ground silhouette with gentle hills anchors the scene. The overall aesthetic is atmospheric, natural, and breathtaking — like viewing the northern lights in the Arctic.

Model: Qwen3.8-27B-instruct
Thinking effort: high

Techniques Used:
- Canvas multi-band aurora rendering with dual sine/sine-wave combination (two independent frequencies per band plus a vertical oscillation term)
- Filled aurora shapes with linear gradient transparency (HSL color stops fading to zero alpha)
- Layered bands (2–4 sub-layers per band) with offset Y positions, amplitude scaling, and phase offsets for volumetric depth
- Additive blending (globalCompositeOperation = 'lighter') for luminous overlap between bands
- Star field with per-star twinkle speed/phase and subtle drift
- Ground silhouette built from stacked sinusoidal hill curves with increasing darkness toward foreground
- Trailing background fade (low-alpha fill each frame) for smooth motion persistence
- HSL color cycling via a global hueShift that eases toward a new target on regeneration
- Click-to-regenerate interaction: new random band configuration, colors, and hill geometry
