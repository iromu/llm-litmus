# 038 — Iridescent Butterfly Wings

## Creative Prompt
Create an iridescent butterfly wing visualization with multiple butterflies floating in space. Each butterfly has two upper and two lower wings rendered with bezier curves, filled with multi-hue radial gradients that shift over time. Wing veins and decorative spots add detail. The wings flap with a sine-wave animation. The butterflies gently drift, and one follows the mouse cursor. Clicking regenerates the butterflies with new colors. Tiny scale-like particles float in the background. The overall aesthetic is delicate, iridescent, and ethereal — like a collection of jewel-toned butterflies.

## Techniques used
- Canvas 2D bezier-curve wing shapes (upper + lower wings, mirrored per side)
- Multi-stop radial gradients (5 stops) for iridescent wing fills, plus linear-gradient sheen bands sweeping across each wing
- HSL color cycling over time: base hue + per-zone hue offsets shifted by a per-butterfly shimmer speed, so every butterfly's palette drifts independently
- Quadratic-curve wing vein strokes and glowing outline strokes
- Decorative spot circles (ring + solid variants) with radial-gradient glow
- Sine-based wing flapping via x-axis fold scaling with slight per-side asymmetry
- Mouse-following butterfly using frame-rate-independent exponential interpolation, orientation toward movement, and a fading particle trail
- Lissajous-style gentle drift for ambient butterflies with soft screen wrap
- Click-to-regenerate: all butterflies get fresh palettes, spots, sizes, and flap speeds
- Floating dust/scale particles with twinkle, plus trailing background fade (translucent indigo wash) for motion trails
- DPR-aware canvas sizing for crisp rendering on retina displays; responsive swarm count on small screens
