Origami — Paper Cranes in Suspension
=====================================

Creative prompt:
Create a 3D origami paper crane scene with multiple cranes floating in space.
Each crane is built from CSS 3D-transformed triangular faces (wings, body, tail,
neck) with warm paper tones. Smaller paper "petals" drift and rotate around them
with floating animations. The entire group rotates slowly, creating a sense of
weightlessness. The background is warm cream/beige. The title "Origami" appears
faintly at the bottom. The overall aesthetic is delicate, handcrafted, and serene
— like paper sculptures suspended in air.

Model: Qwen3.8-27B-instruct
Thinking effort: high

Techniques used:
- CSS 3D transforms with transform-style: preserve-3d on the flock, each crane,
  and every petal; perspective stage with perspective-origin for depth.
- clip-path polygons form the triangular paper faces (diamond body, swept wings,
  neck/head triangles, beak, tail), layered with per-face gradients across a
  warm washi/honey/caramel palette so folds catch light differently.
- Seven cranes positioned in 3D via per-instance custom properties (--cx/--cy/
  --cz/--cs/--ry/--rz) with staggered bobbing keyframes; wing-flap keyframes
  animate rotateX about the wing roots.
- Twelve drifting petals use dual keyframe animation (drift + tumble) driven by
  custom properties for position, size, duration, rotation axes, and opacity.
- Whole-flock slow 90s rotateY spin for weightlessness; soft blurred ground
  shadow ellipse; faint letter-spaced serif title.
- Responsive scaling via vmin stage, clamp() typography, media-query scale
  factors, and prefers-reduced-motion support. No external dependencies.
