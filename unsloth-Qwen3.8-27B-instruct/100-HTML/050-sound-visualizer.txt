# 050 — Sound Visualizer

## Creative Prompt

Create a generative audio visualization with frequency bars that pulse and dance to simulated music data. 64 bars rise and fall with colored gradients (warm to cool spectrum). Each bar has a glow effect and top highlight. A mirrored reflection appears below the baseline. The simulated audio has beats, frequency peaks, and noise bursts that create a realistic visualization feel. The background fades with a trail. The overall aesthetic is vibrant, rhythmic, and energetic — like a premium music player visualizer.

## Generation Metadata

- **Model:** Qwen3.8-27B-instruct
- **Thinking effort:** high

## Techniques Used

- Canvas 2D bar rendering with per-bar vertical linear gradients (brighter at the tip, darker at the base)
- Simulated audio spectrum: 5 layered drifting sine oscillators (spatial + temporal), a 118 BPM beat envelope with exponential decay and downbeat bass kicks, Gaussian low-frequency bias, off-beat mid-range lift, treble shimmer, and scheduled Gaussian noise bursts with sin-shaped envelopes
- Attack/release smoothing on bar heights for a punchy, non-synthetic feel; per-bar random jitter
- Dynamic hue mapping warm (red/orange) → cool (blue/violet) across the spectrum with slow global drift and per-bar phase offsets
- Glow via radial gradients centered above each bar tip; thin bright top-highlight caps
- Mirrored reflection below the baseline (compressed, fading gradient)
- Trailing background fade (translucent dark fill instead of clear) plus a soft vignette
- Responsive layout: DPR-aware canvas sizing, bar width/gap recomputed on resize, baseline positioned to leave room for reflections
