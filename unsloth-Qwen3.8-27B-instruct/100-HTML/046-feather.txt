Creative Prompt:
Create a delicate feather visualization with multiple feathers floating and rotating gently in space. Each feather has a central shaft (rachis), left and right barbs with quadratic curves, and tiny barbules (side hairs). The feathers drift slowly with sine-wave motion. Colors are in the blue-cyan-green spectrum with subtle hue shifting. Fluff at the base adds realism. Clicking regenerates the feathers. The background is very dark with trailing fade. The overall aesthetic is soft, organic, and airy — like feathers suspended in a gentle breeze.

Model: Qwen3.8-27B-instruct
Thinking effort: high

Techniques used:
- Canvas 2D rendering of each feather as layered strokes: a curved rachis (sine-bent polyline), per-side barbs drawn as quadratic Bézier curves with taper toward tip/base, and fine barbule sub-strokes along selected barbs for hair-like detail.
- Fluff tuft at the feather base rendered as radial quadratic strands with slow oscillation.
- Sine-based drift physics: horizontal sway + vertical bob + angular wobble, all phase-offset per feather; screen-edge wrapping with margin.
- HSL color cycling within the blue→cyan→green band (hue base 165–215°) with per-feather drift speed and per-barb shimmer for an iridescent quality.
- Multi-feather composition with randomized geometry, scale/depth layering (smaller = blurred via ctx.filter for distance), back-to-front draw order.
- Trailing fade achieved by painting a low-alpha dark rect each frame instead of clearing.
- Click handler regenerates the entire flock with fresh random parameters; resize re-seeds layout.
- DPR-aware canvas sizing (capped at 2x) for crisp rendering on HiDPI displays.
