The Philosophy
01 / MANIFESTO

Raw Material First

Brutalism rejects ornament the way concrete rejects polish. Every element on this page exists because it has a structural job: the grid carries the layout, the weight carries the voice, the noise carries the grain of the machine that rendered it. Nothing here is decorative. Nothing here is soft.

The three title lines are set in descending confidence — filled, hollow, filled again — a rhythm of assertion and outline that mirrors how brutalist architecture alternates mass and void. The glitch is not an effect; it is the signal degrading honestly in front of you.

02 / METHOD

Confrontation by Design

A brutalist page does not guide the eye gently. It slams scale against whitespace, pits red against blue, and lets monospace labels do the accounting work while display faces shout. The stats grid below the title is deliberately mechanical — bars that fill, pulse, and report numbers like instruments on a control panel.

The noise overlay and the scanning line keep the surface alive in the way CRT phosphor stayed alive: imperfectly, continuously, analogically. Click anywhere and the glitch re-arms. The page prefers intensity to comfort, and it will keep asking for yours.
