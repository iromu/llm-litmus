Creative Prompt
---------------
Create a soft bokeh circle visualization with large blurred colored orbs drifting gently across a dark gradient background. Each circle has a radial gradient with a brighter inner spot, simulating lens bokeh. Circles have various sizes, blur amounts, alpha levels, and hue/saturation values, creating a rich, dreamlike depth of field effect. They drift slowly with sine-wave acceleration and wrap around screen edges. Clicking spawns a burst of new circles at the cursor position with explosive velocity. The title "Bokeh Dreams" is rendered in faint overlay text. The overall feel is calm, dreamy, and photographic.

Techniques Used

- Canvas 2D rendering with multi-stop radial gradients (6 stops) for lens-like light falloff

- Phase-based sine/cosine drift acceleration for organic motion
- Screen-edge wrapping with margin compensation
- Click-to-burst spawning with outward radial velocity and exponential decay
- Hue variety across 6 curated color ranges (warm amber, soft red, cool blue, violet, yellow, green)
- Limited circle count (200 max) with oldest-circle replacement strategy
- Animated dark gradient background with slow hue shift
- CSS vignette overlay for photographic depth
- SVG noise grain overlay for film texture
- Faint serif title with breathing opacity animation
- Device-pixel-ratio aware canvas sizing (capped at 2x)
