# Sound Visualizer

## Creative Prompt
Create a generative audio frequency bar visualization. A series of vertical bars animate with simulated frequency data, creating a music visualizer effect. Bars have gradient fills, glow effects, and smooth animation. The visualization includes a central waveform display, circular frequency rings, and particle effects. The color palette is vibrant neon (cyan, magenta, purple). The overall aesthetic is energetic, colorful, and rhythmic — like a music visualizer at a concert.

## Techniques Used
- HTML5 Canvas frequency bar rendering with gradient fills
- Simulated audio data with multi-layered sine wave generation
- Waveform display with smooth bezier curves and gradient stroke
- Circular frequency rings with radial symmetry and counter-rotation
- Particle effects that spawn on frequency peaks with gravity
- Neon color palette (cyan → magenta → purple) with per-bar color mapping
- Bar height transitions driven by continuous sine-based simulation
- Mirror reflection bars beneath the main frequency display
- Canvas shadow blur for neon glow effect on bars, waveform, and rings
- Subtle background grid for depth
- Alpha-based trailing effect for motion blur
- Responsive canvas filling the full viewport

## Interaction Model
Passive animation — bars pulse, waveform flows, rings rotate, and particles react to frequency peaks. The visualization simulates a music track with varying intensity over time. No user interaction required.
