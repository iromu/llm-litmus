# Ink Bleed

## Creative Prompt
Create a watercolor ink spreading effect on paper. Ink drops fall onto a textured paper surface and spread outward with organic, branching patterns. The ink has color variations (deep blue, burgundy, forest green) and creates realistic bleeding effects with capillary-like branching. The paper has a subtle texture and warm tone. Clicking adds new ink drops. The overall aesthetic is artistic, organic, and painterly — like watching ink spread on watercolor paper.

## Techniques Used
- Grid-based ink diffusion simulation at reduced resolution
- Multi-octave sine noise for paper resistance field
- Channel noise for capillary-like branching patterns
- Concentration-dependent diffusion rate with gradient-based flow
- Per-channel (R/G/B) independent ink diffusion
- Alpha compositing with power-law concentration mapping
- Canvas `multiply` blend mode for ink-on-paper darkening
- Procedural paper texture with noise grain, fiber streaks, and vignette
- Scaled render canvas with high-quality bilinear interpolation
- Click interaction with randomized ink color selection

## Interaction Model
Click anywhere on the canvas to drop a new ink blob at that position. Each drop randomly selects from four ink colors (deep blue, burgundy, forest green, charcoal). The ink diffuses outward following the noise-based resistance field, creating organic branching patterns. Multiple drops overlap and blend, producing rich color mixing effects. Three initial drops appear at page load to demonstrate the effect.
