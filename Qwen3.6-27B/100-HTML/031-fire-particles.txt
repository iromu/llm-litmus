# Fire Particles

## Creative Prompt
Create a fire particle simulation with glowing embers that rise, flicker, and fade. Particles spawn at the mouse cursor, creating a controlled flame effect. Each particle has warm hues (orange, red, yellow), upward velocity with wobble, a radial glow, and a bright core. Particles decay in life, size, and alpha. Clicking creates a burst of fire. The background has a dark red-black trailing fade. The overall aesthetic is warm, dynamic, and fiery — like a living flame.

## Techniques Used
- Canvas particle system with upward acceleration
- Wobble via sine function for natural drift
- Multi-hue fire coloring (HSL 10-50 range)
- Radial gradient glow with bright core rendering
- Life-cycle management with per-particle decay rate
- Mouse-follow spawning with interpolated path steps
- Click burst spawning (80 particles)
- Auto-spawn at bottom for ambient fire sources
- Particle pool limit (2000 max)
- Trailing background with semi-transparent dark red-black fill
- DPR-aware canvas for crisp rendering on Retina displays
- Touch support for mobile interaction

## Interaction Model
Move mouse to create fire along the path. Click for a burst of flames. Fire rises naturally with ambient spawns at the bottom of the canvas. Touch and drag on mobile to create fire.
