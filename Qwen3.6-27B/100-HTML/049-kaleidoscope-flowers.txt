# Kaleidoscope Flowers

## Creative Prompt
Create blooming floral kaleidoscope patterns. Flower shapes bloom from the center with radial symmetry, each petal rendered as a bezier curve with gradient fills. Multiple flower layers bloom at different speeds and sizes. The petals have vibrant colors that shift through the spectrum. The background is dark with a subtle glow. The overall aesthetic is beautiful, colorful, and organic — like a time-lapse of flowers blooming.

## Techniques Used
- Canvas flower rendering with bezier petal shapes
- Radial symmetry with multiple petal counts
- Bloom animation with scale and rotation
- Gradient fills with HSL color cycling
- Multiple flower layers with independent parameters
- Petal overlap with alpha blending and additive compositing
- Center glow with pulsing radial gradient
- Continuous bloom and fade cycle
- Floating pollen particles with orbital motion
- Background glow rings
- Responsive canvas with DPR scaling

## Interaction Model
Passive animation — flowers bloom, rotate, and fade continuously. Each flower layer has independent timing for bloom speed, rotation, color shift, and phase offset.
