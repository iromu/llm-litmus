# Night Sky

## Creative Prompt
Create an interactive constellation viewer. A star field with thousands of stars of varying brightness and color. Constellation patterns are overlaid with connecting lines and labels. Clicking on a constellation highlights it and shows information. The Milky Way is visible as a band of denser stars. The overall aesthetic is scientific, beautiful, and educational — like a star chart come to life.

## Techniques Used
- Canvas star field rendering with thousands of procedurally generated stars
- Star color temperature variation (blue-white to orange-red)
- Bright star glow halos with radial gradients
- Star twinkling animation with per-star phase and frequency
- Milky Way band with denser star distribution and subtle nebula glow
- Constellation line rendering with multi-layer glow effect
- Point-to-segment distance calculation for click hit testing
- Smooth constellation highlighting with selection and hover states
- Information display overlay with backdrop blur and gradient text
- Dark sky radial gradient background
- Responsive canvas with DPR-aware rendering

## Interaction Model
Click on a constellation to highlight it and display its name, Latin designation, description, star count, and brightest magnitude. Click the same constellation again or click empty sky to deselect. Hovering over a constellation changes the cursor and partially highlights it. Stars twinkle continuously with independent phases.
