# Space Explorer

## Creative Prompt
Create an interactive 3D space exploration scene with a star field and various space objects (planets with optional rings, asteroids, nebula clouds). The camera moves forward continuously through space at a variable speed controlled by scroll wheel. Mouse drag rotates the view. Stars are distributed in a large volume and regenerate as they pass behind. Planets have radial gradient shading with ring ellipses. The UI shows position coordinates, star count, speed, and object count in a HUD-like overlay. The aesthetic is deep space, immersive, and exploratory.

## Techniques Used
- Canvas 3D projection with rotation matrices (Y then X) and perspective scaling
- Star field with depth-based sizing, twinkling, and color variation (blue/white/warm)
- Planetary rendering with multi-stop radial gradients and atmospheric glow halos
- Ring ellipses with rotated coordinate transforms and radial gradient strokes
- Asteroid rendering with procedurally generated irregular polygon vertices
- Nebula clouds as large semi-transparent radial gradient overlays
- Mouse drag rotation with yaw/pitch limits for view control
- Scroll wheel speed control with smooth acceleration/deceleration
- Star field regeneration for infinite forward travel illusion
- Depth-sorted planet rendering for correct occlusion
- HUD overlay with monospace typography and glow text-shadow
- Speed bar indicator with gradient fill
- Deep space radial gradient background
- Touch support for mobile drag rotation

## Interaction Model
Drag to rotate view (mouse or touch). Scroll to change forward speed (0.1× to 10×). Camera moves forward continuously through space, creating a sense of space travel. Stars regenerate ahead of the camera as old ones pass behind. Planets, asteroids, and nebulae are encountered and passed during travel. The HUD displays real-time position coordinates, visible object count, total star count, and current speed multiplier.
