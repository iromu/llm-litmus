# Rose

## Creative Prompt
Create an animated blooming rose visualization. A rose flower blooms petal by petal, with each petal unfurling from a tight bud. The petals have gradient fills in shades of red, pink, and cream. The stem grows upward with leaves unfurling. The animation loops continuously — blooming, holding, and closing. The background is soft and romantic. The overall aesthetic is beautiful, organic, and elegant — like a time-lapse of a rose blooming.

## Techniques Used
- Canvas rose rendering with bezier petal shapes
- Petal-by-petal bloom animation with staggered timing
- Gradient fills with red/pink/cream palette
- Stem and leaf rendering with bezier curves
- Bloom and close cycle animation (18-second loop)
- Gentle sway for organic movement
- Dew drop rendering with radial gradients
- Sepal, stamen, and petal vein details
- Floating pollen particles in background
- Soft radial glow behind the rose
- Responsive canvas with DPR scaling

## Interaction Model
Passive animation — the rose blooms, holds, and closes in a continuous 18-second cycle. The stem grows first, followed by leaves unfurling, then petals opening layer by layer from outer to inner. After holding full bloom, the rose gently closes back to a bud and the cycle repeats.
