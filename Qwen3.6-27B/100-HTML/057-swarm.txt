# Swarm

## Creative Prompt
Create a boids flocking simulation. Hundreds of triangular agents move across the screen following three simple rules: separation (avoid crowding), alignment (steer toward average heading of neighbors), and cohesion (move toward the center of nearby flockmates). The result is emergent flocking behavior that looks like a school of fish or flock of birds. The boids have a subtle glow and trail effect. The background is dark. The overall aesthetic is organic, mesmerizing, and computational — like watching emergent behavior.

## Techniques Used
- HTML5 Canvas boids simulation with separation/alignment/cohesion rules
- Spatial grid optimization for O(n) neighbor search
- Velocity steering behaviors with speed clamping
- Triangular boid rendering with heading-based rotation
- Glow effects via canvas shadow blur
- Trail rendering with alpha-fade background overlay
- Mouse interaction with attract/repel modes
- HSL color variation across agents for organic feel
- Edge wrapping for seamless boundaries
- Touch support for mobile devices
- Responsive canvas filling the viewport

## Interaction Model
Boids flock continuously using emergent behavior rules. Mouse movement influences boid behavior — boids either flee from or move toward the cursor. Click to toggle between repel (default) and attract modes. A mode indicator in the top-right corner shows the current interaction mode.
