# Circuit Board

## Creative Prompt
Create a glowing green circuit board visualization on a dark green background. Nodes are arranged in a semi-random grid connected by traces (lines) with pulsing signal dots traveling along them. Traces glow with a green neon color and fade in and out with pulse animation. Nodes have a similar glow and pulse. The scene has a trailing fade for smooth animation. The title "Circuit Board" appears faintly at the bottom in green. The overall aesthetic is cybernetic, technological, and neon-green — like a living PCB.

## Techniques Used
- Canvas trace rendering with pulse-driven alpha modulation
- Traveling pulse dots along line segments
- Grid-based node placement with random perturbation
- Connection logic based on distance threshold
- Radial gradient node glow with breathing animation
- Exponential background fade for motion trail
- Sinusoidal pulse function for organic signal feel
- Bent and straight trace routing for PCB realism

## Interaction Model
Purely passive — traces pulse continuously with traveling signal dots. Nodes breathe with intensity. The pattern is generated fresh on resize.
