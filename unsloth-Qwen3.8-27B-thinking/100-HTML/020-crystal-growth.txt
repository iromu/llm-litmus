Crystal Growth (020)
====================

CREATIVE PROMPT (verbatim)
--------------------------
Create a generative crystal growth visualization where faceted crystal structures grow organically from nucleation points. Each crystal has multiple facets radiating from a center, rendered as quadrilateral shapes with varying hues (blue-purple spectrum), alpha levels, and edge strokes. Crystal tips have a glow effect. Crystals slowly grow additional facets over time. Clicking spawns new crystals at the cursor. Moving the mouse also generates new crystals in the vicinity. The background fades with a trailing effect. The overall aesthetic is frozen, geometric, and luminescent — like observing crystal formation under a microscope.

MODEL
-----
Qwen3.8-27B (thinking) — thinking effort: high

TECHNIQUES
----------
- Canvas 2D quadrilateral facet rendering (each facet a tapered lozenge radiating from a nucleation center)
- Organic growth algorithm with a per-crystal generation limit (facets added over time up to maxGen)
- HSL color cycling per facet, clamped to the blue-purple spectrum (hue 185-305)
- Tip glow via pre-rendered radial-gradient sprites drawn with additive "lighter" compositing
- Mouse-following crystal generation (distance- and time-throttled seeding along the cursor path)
- Trailing background via semi-transparent fillRect each frame
- Click-driven cluster spawning
- Population control capped at MAX = 120 crystals with graceful fade-out of the oldest
- requestAnimationFrame loop, devicePixelRatio-capped responsive canvas, touch support

INTERACTION MODEL
-----------------
- Click: nucleate a crystal cluster at the cursor (4-6 satellites + a central seed)
- Move mouse / drag on touch: grow new crystals along the path (throttled by distance + time)
- Passive: crystals slowly add facets over time and drift-rotate; ambient nucleation keeps the field alive
- Scene evolves continuously under a trailing fade; HUD shows live crystal count, facet total, and fps
