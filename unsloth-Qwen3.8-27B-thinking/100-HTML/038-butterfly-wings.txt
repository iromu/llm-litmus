Creative Prompt (verbatim)
=========================

Create an iridescent butterfly wing visualization with multiple butterflies floating in space. Each butterfly has two upper and two lower wings rendered with bezier curves, filled with multi-hue radial gradients that shift over time. Wing veins and decorative spots add detail. The wings flap with a sine-wave animation. The butterflies gently drift, and one follows the mouse cursor. Clicking regenerates the butterflies with new colors. Tiny scale-like particles float in the background. The overall aesthetic is delicate, iridescent, and ethereal — like a collection of jewel-toned butterflies.

=====

Model: Qwen3.8-27B (thinking) — thinking effort: high

Techniques Used
===============

Canvas-2D renders a swarm of six butterflies (five free-flying + one mouse-follower) over a field of dust motes, all in a single inline self-contained page. Each butterfly is a mirrored pair of bezier-wing silhouettes in local space (origin at the thorax, x+ out the right side): the upper wing is moveTo(0, -0.02L) → bezierCurveTo(0.12L, -0.72L, 0.78L, -1.02L, 1.02L, -0.42L) → bezierCurveTo(1.1L, -0.1L, 0.5L, 0.16L, 0, 0.08L), and the lower wing is moveTo(0, 0.06L) → bezierCurveTo(0.42L, 0.14L, 0.72L, 0.5L, 0.6L, 0.98L) → bezierCurveTo(0.28L, 1.06L, 0.06L, 0.66L, 0, 0.06L). Each wing is filled with a 5-stop radial gradient centred at (0.18L, -0.1L) with outer radius 1.25L: a hot hsla(c1+30, 95, 78) core stepping through c1, c1-45, c1-90 and ending in a dark hsla(c1-120, 78, 26) rim, where c1 = hue + t*40 so the whole palette cycles in HSL over time — the upper wing drives on hueA and the lower on hueB (a per-butterfly warm/cool offset). drawWingPair mirrors the left half with scale(-1, 1) and folds both wings about the body axis via scale(flap, 1), lays a soft shadowBlur glow (L*0.5 for the upper, L*0.4 for the lower) before each fill, and re-strokes a lighter hsla(hue+150, 80, ~83) outline (lineWidth ~L*0.014). Wing veins are 3 upper + 2 lower quadratic curves drawn in rgba(255,255,255,0.30) (lineWidth max(0.7, L*0.012), round caps), and 4 decorative spots at fixed local coordinates are each painted three times — a main hsla(h, 85, 82) dot, a brighter hsla(h+40, 95, 94) inner highlight at 0.55r, and an hsla(h, 90, 88) halo ring at 1.35r. The body (drawBody) sits on top of the wings: a vertical ellipse (0.05L × 0.4L) filled with a 3-stop vertical linear gradient in hueBase (+0/+30/+50, lightness 34→22→14), a head circle at (0, -0.38L) r 0.062L, and two mirrored antennae — quadratic curves arcing out to (±0.2L, -0.52L) with a glowing hsla(hueBase+120, 80, 80) tip dot at r 0.014L. Wing flapping is a sine: flap = 0.55 + 0.45*sin(t*flapSpeed + flapPhase) so the X-scale oscillates 0.10–1.00 at per-butterfly flapSpeed 2.1–3.3 rad/s; each butterfly also bobs vertically (sin(t*0.8 + bobPhase) * bobAmp, bobAmp 6–16 px) and tilts ±0.08 rad. makeDust seeds the background with round(clamp(W*H/16000, 50, 140)) drifting motes (radius 0.5–1.9, upward vy of -0.03…-0.12, hue hueBase+160 ± 40) that twinkle on 0.5 + 0.5*sin(t*sp + ph) (sp 0.15–0.5) and wrap at the edges. The canvas is DPR-aware (DPR capped at 2), uses a trailing-fade paintBase (fillRect rgba(6,9,18,0.26) each frame instead of a full clear, so fast wingbeats leave short luminous trails), and the body carries a layered CSS background — two radial colour washes rgba(64,46,128,0.45)/rgba(24,84,108,0.4) over a #05070f → #0a0e22 → #070a18 vertical gradient — with cursor:crosshair. A pointer-events:none DOM HUD overlays the scene: a weight-300, 0.32em-tracked "BUTTERFLY WINGS" title in gradient text (linear-gradient 92deg #9be7ff → #d6b3ff → #ffb3e6, background-clip:text, 24px purple text-shadow) with a "Jewel-toned · Iridescent · Ethereal" subline, and a centred pill hint ("Drift with the cursor · Click to metamorphose") pulsing on a 4.5s opacity cycle, all in the Segoe UI / system stack. Bootstrap is resize() → regenerate() → makeDust() → requestAnimationFrame(frame); every frame is t = (now - start)/1000 in seconds.

Interaction Model
=================

The mouse-follower butterfly (makeButterfly(follow=true), starts at screen centre, sized min(W,H)*0.075 clamped 30–52 px) eases toward the cursor at 0.09/frame on x and y, and its bank angle relaxes back toward 0 at 0.06/frame; mousemove and passive touchmove both feed the same target (before first input the target is held at screen centre by a !mouse.active guard in resize). The five free butterflies (26–50 px, random x in [0.15,0.85]*W and y in [0.2,0.8]*H, per-butterfly hueA rand(0,360)/hueB rand(40,110), initial drift speed ≤0.25 px/frame) wander organically: per-frame sinusoidal acceleration (sin(t*0.5+wander)*0.004 on x, cos(t*0.4+1.3*wander)*0.003 on y with wander advancing 0.005/frame), 0.995 velocity damping, soft reflection at a margin of 0.9*size, and a banked angle that lerps toward clamp(-vx*0.3, ±0.35) at 0.05/frame so they lean into their motion. Clicking anywhere (click on window) calls regenerate(), which re-rolls the global hueBase and rebuilds the entire swarm with a fresh random palette — the "click to metamorphose" behaviour the hint pill advertises. resize re-fits the canvas (DPR-scaled), re-seeds the dust count from the new viewport area, and re-centers an idle mouse target. Under prefers-reduced-motion the page settles into a calm still: the trailing fade becomes a full opaque clear (paintBase(1)), updateButterflies is skipped entirely (no drift, no following lerp), flapping is frozen at a fixed 0.78 fold with zero bob and tilt, the HSL hue cycle is slowed 10x (t*0.1), dust positions are frozen (drift guarded off) while twinkle slows to 0.3x, the swarm shrinks to 4 free + 1 follower, and the CSS .hint pulse is disabled (animation:none; opacity:0.85) — only the slow colour shimmer and dust twinkle remain.
