DOC.007-01 / PHILOSOPHY

The Logic Of Raw Type

BRUTAL TYPE IS NOT A STYLE. IT IS A POSTURE. Every glyph is set at maximum weight, every margin at maximum tension. We do not decorate. We do not compromise. The letterform is a structure of concrete and steel, and it shows its joints.

Worn originally by the post-war movement, brutalism rejected the polite surfaces of decorative design in favor of honest, unfinished material. This page is the same idea rendered in pixels: no border radius, no soft shadows, no permission. If it hurts a little to look at, it is working.
